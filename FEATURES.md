# Features

## MDX Support
Write your content using Markdown with the power of React components. This allows for rich, interactive essays.

## Audio Player
A custom audio player is integrated into the blog. It supports:
- Play/Pause functionality
- Progress bar
- Time display
- Persistent playback across navigation (if configured with a global context)

## Filtering
The homepage features a "Selected Essays" section where you can curate which posts are displayed.

## Navigation
Simple and intuitive navigation bar and footer to help visitors explore your content and connect with you.

## SEO
Built-in SEO optimization ensures your blog posts are indexed correctly by search engines.

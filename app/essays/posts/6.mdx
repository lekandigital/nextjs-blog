Drinking water is essential for life. Our bodies are composed of approximately 60% water. Staying hydrated affects energy levels, brain function, and physical performance.

## Methods of Consumption

1.  **The Glass**: Classic, refined.
2.  **The Bottle**: Portable, convenient.
3.  **The Fountain**: Public, accessible.

## When to Drink

It is recommended to drink water throughout the day, not just when you feel thirsty. Thirst is often a late sign of dehydration.

> **Fact**: Water has zero calories, making it the healthiest beverage choice.

## Conclusion

Drink more water. Your body will thank you.

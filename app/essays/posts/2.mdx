Frying an egg is a fundamental culinary skill. While it seems simple, achieving the perfect balance of cooked white and runny yolk requires attention to detail.

## Ingredients Needed

- 1 Fresh Egg
- Butter or Oil
- Salt and Pepper

## The Process

1.  **Heat the Pan**: Place a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add your fat (butter or oil) and let it melt.
2.  **Crack the Egg**: Carefully crack the egg into the center of the pan. Try not to break the yolk.
3.  **Cook**: Let the egg cook undisturbed. The whites will turn from clear to opaque.
4.  **Season**: Sprinkle with salt and pepper.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve your fried egg on toast, over rice, or as a side to bacon. The rich yolk acts as a sauce, adding moisture and flavor to the dish.

"Cooking" cereal is a misnomer, yet the preparation of a bowl of Frosted Flakes is an art form in itself. It requires precision, timing, and the right ratio of ingredients.

## The Debate: Milk or Cereal First?

This is a contentious topic.
- **Cereal First**: Allows you to see how much you are eating. Minimizes splash.
- **Milk First**: Keeps the cereal crunchier for longer. (Controversial)

## Instructions

1.  Retrieve a clean bowl.
2.  Pour Frosted Flakes into the bowl.
3.  Add cold milk until the flakes are just beginning to float.
4.  **Eat immediately**.

## Texture Analysis

The window of peak enjoyment is small. You want the flakes to be cold and crunchy, but coated in milk. Wait too long, and you are left with a soggy mush.

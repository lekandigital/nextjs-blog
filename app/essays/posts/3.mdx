There is nothing quite as refreshing as a glass of ice water on a hot day. The process of making it is simple, yet the result is profound.

## The Components

You need two things:
1.  Liquid Water (H2O)
2.  Solid Water (Ice)

## The Method

Fill a glass with ice cubes. Pour water over the ice. Wait a few moments for the thermal equilibrium to shift, cooling the liquid water.

![Glass of Water](/images/3-1.jpeg)

## Why It Works

The phase change of ice melting absorbs heat from the surrounding water, lowering its temperature. This physical process ensures your drink stays cold for a prolonged period.

> **Tip**: Use filtered water for the cleanest taste.

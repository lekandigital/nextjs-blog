The question "Is water wet?" has sparked countless debates on the internet. To answer it, we must define what "wet" means.

## The Definition

**Wetness** is typically defined as the ability of a liquid to adhere to the surface of a solid.

## The Arguments

### Argument: Water is Not Wet
Water itself is a liquid. Wetness is a description of our experience of water. You get wet when water touches you. Water cannot touch itself in a way that makes it "wet".

### Argument: Water is Wet
Water molecules are surrounded by other water molecules. Since they are in contact with a liquid, they are, by definition, wet.

## Conclusion

It depends on your semantic definition. Scientifically, wetness is an interaction between a liquid and a solid surface. So, strictly speaking, water is not wet; it makes other things wet.
